To My Sister ❤️

I don't know if these words will ever be enough, but every one of them comes from my heart.

I'm truly, deeply sorry.

I know I hurt you, and I have no excuse for the way I acted. I let my own emotions, frustrations, and selfishness come before someone who has always cared about me.

Looking back, I'm ashamed of the pain I caused you. You have always been more than just my sister. You've supported me, cared for me, and stood beside me even when I didn't deserve it.

Instead of appreciating that, I hurt you. I reacted without thinking, spoke without kindness, and made things harder for you when I should have been making them easier.

I'm sorry for every hurtful word, every selfish decision, and every moment I disappointed you. I should have listened instead of reacting, understood instead of getting angry, and thought about your feelings before my own.

You deserved a better brother. I became someone who caused you pain, and I regret that more than I can ever put into words.

I know saying "I'm sorry" doesn't erase the past, and I don't expect you to forgive me immediately. If you're still angry or hurt, I completely understand.

I simply want you to know that my apology is genuine. I can't change yesterday, but I can choose to become a better brother tomorrow.

No matter how long it takes, I'll always hope that one day we can heal, laugh together again, and create better memories than the painful ones I caused.

I'm truly, deeply, and sincerely sorry. ❤️

With all my love,

Your Brother ❤️

If someday you find it in your heart to forgive me... I'll be here.
